# UseBy — Design brief

## Feeling
A calm kitchen counter at golden hour: paper recipes, sage tile, one coral sticky note that says “cook me first.” Hand-crafted, not SaaS dashboard. Not purple AI-slop gradients.

## References → visual moves
- Samsung Food / Family Hub → **fridge dashboard** with clear use-by hierarchy
- SuperCook → **“you can make this”** coverage language
- Too Good To Go → **gentle urgency color**, human microcopy
- Indie food blogs (Not Quite Nigella energy) → **serif titles + friendly sans body**

## Palette
- Cream paper `#F4EFE4`, card `#FFFAF2`, ink `#1D2A24`
- Sage primary `#2F5D4A`, sage soft `#DCE9E1`
- Coral urgency `#C45C3E`, amber soon `#C9922A`
- No purple. Soft shadows only; cards feel like paper, not glass.

## Type
- **Fraunces** for titles (slightly italic emphasis on key phrases)
- **DM Sans** for UI / body
- Microcopy short: “Use tonight”, “2 days left”, “Why this now”

## Layout (mobile-first)
- Sticky top: logo + 3 tabs (Fridge / Cook / Learn)
- Fridge: hero CTA → urgency strip → sorted item cards
- Cook: stacked recipe cards with priority ribbon + why line
- Empty states are illustrated with purpose + one primary button (never blank gray)
- Dialogs for add/edit and recipe detail; cook mode uses large step pager

## Signature interaction
**Priority ribbon** on recipes: “Cook tonight — spinach hits in 1 day” — the product’s thesis in one line.
