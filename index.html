How UseBy works

Architecture (ready for real vision)

  1. VisionProvider — mock detector today; plug in Claude Vision / GPT-4o / custom model tomorrow.
  2. ShelfLife — maps food categories to typical fridge days; you can edit any date.
  3. RecipeEngine — scores recipes by days-left of matched ingredients (sooner = higher priority).
  4. localStorage — your fridge persists in the browser; no backend required for the MVP.
